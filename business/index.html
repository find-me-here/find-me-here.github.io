FAQ

Questions, before you start.

What can I run from Business?

An online store, a news site and your social pages. Each has its own screens and its own menu, and the Business overview shows how many of each you have.

How do customers pay in the online store?

By cash on delivery or by bank transfer, with the details to send it to shown once they have ordered. Online card and wallet payments are not part of the store yet.

Do I need a developer or hosting for a news site?

No. Pick a newsroom template, arrange the front page block by block and publish. Stories are written so that search engines can read them from the first day.

Which social networks can I post to?

Facebook Pages today, with text, pictures and video. Other networks are planned but not built.

Is my Facebook login safe?

The connection and the publishing happen on our server: your browser never talks to Facebook’s API and never sees an access token.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Everything you run is kept in your account, so it is there when you come back.
