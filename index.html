FAQ

Questions, before you start.

Do I need a website already?

No. Picking a name is the whole setup — your page is live at Mero Page/yourname straight away, and you fill it in from there.

What can the AI do?

On a site, describe a page in a sentence or two and it lays out the blocks, or ask it to rewrite a single block. On a résumé, chat with it — “make my summary shorter”, “add a skill” — and it edits the document. Nothing is final: you can undo what it did and change any of it by hand. There are fair-use limits so it stays quick for everyone.

Can I make it look like mine?

Yes. Unlimited page templates, your own colours and background image, and any section can be reordered or hidden. If you want more control, build a site from blocks or write the HTML yourself.

Can I build my own site?

Yes. Start with a template, describe it to the AI, arrange blocks to suit your needs, or write the HTML yourself for complete control.

Can you build one for me?

Yes. Beyond the app, we build custom sites, AI and machine-learning features, and interactive websites for clients. See what we do or send us a message.

How do customers get to it?

Share the link in your bio or a message, or print the QR code for the counter, the table or the window. Both point at the same page, so updating the page updates what everyone sees.

Does it work with eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay?

Your QR page holds the code images those apps give you, alongside the ones generated here — so one printed sheet covers payment, your menu and your link. We don't handle the payment itself; the code opens your wallet app the way it always does.

What if my details change?

Edit them and save. The link and the printed QR code keep working — nothing needs reprinting or resharing.

What are the résumés for?

The same account builds a résumé from 14 templates, edits it with the AI when you ask, shares it as a link, or prints it to PDF — useful if you're the business.
