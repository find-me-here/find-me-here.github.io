Free online games

Play in your browser, on a computer or a phone. Each game opens in a tab of its own.

  • Everest ClimberClimb Everest as a Nepali mountaineer: dodge ice and avalanches, keep your oxygen up and reach the summit.
  • Mero RiderRide a dirt bike over a random market chart: up days are hills, down days are drops. Reach the finish flag.
  • Nepal RideRide a motorcycle on the real roads of Pokhara: Lakeside, the hairpins to Sarangkot, the Peace Pagoda — with the Annapurnas ahead.
  • Nepali Ludo & SnakesLudo and Snake & Ladder for 2–4 players, and Tic-Tac-Toe for two — against friends or the computer, under Nepali skies.
  • PadkaA multiplayer survival shooter on Nepali maps: climb bamboo, throw khukuris, keep your three lives — last one standing wins.
