FAQ

Questions, before you start.

Do I need a website already?

No. Picking a name is the whole setup — your page is live at Mero Page/profile/yourname straight away, and you fill it in from there.

What does a QR code add?

It opens the same page from a phone camera, so you can print it on a business card, a poster or packaging and keep the details in one place.

What do business profiles add?

Each business you run gets a profile page and a QR code of its own, so a customer of one business never lands on the details of another. They come with a pro account.
