FAQ

Questions, before you start.

Do I need a website already?

No. Picking a name is the whole setup — your page is live at www.meropage.com/link/profile/yourname straight away, and you fill it in from there.

What is a link-in-bio page?

A single page you put in the profile of a social account or in a message, which holds your contact details and links so people don’t need several. Your page is that: your phone, WhatsApp, socials, location and hours on one address.

What does a QR code add?

It opens the same page from a phone camera, so you can print it on a business card, a poster or packaging and keep the details in one place.

Can I download my QR code?

Yes. Download it as an image and print it on business cards, posters, menus or packaging.

Can I add my Google reviews and map?

Yes. A page can carry your Google Maps location and a link to your Google reviews beside your phone, WhatsApp and social links.

What do business profiles add?

Each business you run gets a profile page and a QR code of its own, so a customer of one business never lands on the details of another. They come with a pro account.
