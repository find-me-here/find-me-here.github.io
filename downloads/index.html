Save posts from anywhere

The Mero Page extension for Chrome saves a post from Facebook, Instagram, X or any other site to your Captures in one click — with a note, tags and a collection if you want them. It works in Chrome on a computer, and in Edge and Brave too.

Download the extension

Add it to Chrome

  1. Unzip the file you downloaded.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

Connect your account

Once, so what you save lands in your Captures: click the Mero Page icon in Chrome’s toolbar and choose Connect, or use the button below while signed in.

Connect the extension

Saving a post

  1. A post’s own page

    Click the Mero Page icon in Chrome’s toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+S), add a note or tags if you like, and Save.

  2. A post in a feed

    Right-click the post’s time stamp — on Facebook, Instagram and X that is the link to the post — and choose “Save link to Mero Page”.

  3. Just some words

    Select them, right-click, and choose “Save selection to Mero Page”. The page they came from is kept with them.
