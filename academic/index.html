FAQ

Questions, before you start.

Do I need an account to practice reading?

No. Anyone can practice the community’s sets. An account lets you generate your own set from any topic and keep your own libraries.

Which tests is it for?

It is built around Academic Reading practice, the kind of passage-and-questions task that IELTS and PTE use.

Who can see my unknown words?

Only you. Each word is kept in your own list, tied to your account.
