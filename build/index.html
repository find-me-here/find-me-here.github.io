FAQ

Questions, before you start.

Do I need an account to build?

Yes, for the site, résumé and data tools: what you make is saved to your account. The presentation maker also works without one, right in your browser, at Mero Page/presentation-maker.

What can the AI do?

On a site, describe a page in a sentence or two and it lays out the blocks, or ask it to rewrite a single block. On a résumé, chat with it — “make my summary shorter”, “add a skill” — and it edits the document. Nothing is final: you can undo what it did and change any of it by hand.

Can I build my own site?

Yes. Start with a template, describe it to the AI, arrange blocks to suit your needs, or write the HTML yourself for complete control.

Can I download my presentation?

Yes. Present it from the browser, share a view-only link, or download it as a PowerPoint file.
